Frequently asked questions

Where is my journal stored?

On your devices and in your own iCloud account. Daykeep has no account of its own and no server that stores your entries. See the privacy policy.

My journals aren’t syncing between devices

Sync uses iCloud. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Daykeep is allowed to use iCloud in Settings. Sync can take a little while after a lot of new photos; leaving the app open on both devices with a connection usually settles it.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to use, and Daykeep Plus is a one-off purchase. If you had Plus and it is not showing on a new device, use Restore Purchases — it is a Universal Purchase across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

How do I get my writing out of the app?

Back up any journal to a file at any time, and restore your own backups for free. You can also print a journal as a book, or export it to Mosaic Collage Maker for a designed photobook.

How do I turn a journal into a printed book?

Two ways. Print a clean book straight from Daykeep, or use Export for Collage and open the file in Mosaic Collage Maker to build a fully designed photobook. The guide walks through it.

I lost an entry / deleted a journal by mistake

If you have a backup file, you can restore it. This is exactly why Daykeep lets you back up to your own files — keep an occasional backup somewhere safe.
