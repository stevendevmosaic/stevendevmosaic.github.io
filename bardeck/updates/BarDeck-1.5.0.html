BarDeck 1.5.0

New

  • Menu bar mode. Right-click and choose Show BarDeck in to use the floating bar, the menu bar, or both. In the menu bar your buttons appear as a menu in the same colours, with stacks and folders as submenus, volume and brightness sliders, live values and the current app’s buttons.
  • Pin to menu bar. Give any button its own menu bar item, showing its live value, share price or current track. Up to five.
  • Live data buttons show when they last updated (hover over the button), say “stale” when a refresh has failed, and scripts can report their own errors.
