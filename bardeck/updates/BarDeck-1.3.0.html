BarDeck 1.3.0

New

  • Now Playing buttons show the track, artist and cover art from Apple Music, Spotify, Qobuz or a Sonos speaker, and play or pause with a tap.
  • Stock prices: a button for any share or index, with the day’s change and a small chart.
  • Compact or Normal size: every button is one height, and the whole bar can be switched to a larger Normal size from the right-click menu.
  • Licences are now activated online and work on 2 Macs. Licence… in the menu shows your key and lets you deactivate a Mac to move it. Copy and paste now work in every BarDeck text box.
