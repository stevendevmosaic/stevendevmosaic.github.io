BarDeck 1.1

New: controls

  • Right-click the bar and choose Add control to add a volume slider, a brightness slider, play/pause, next and previous track, mute and an emoji picker.
  • Buttons that control Music and Spotify directly.
  • The brightness slider works with the built-in display and Apple's own displays.

New: share your setup

  • Share layout exports your whole layout, or just one stack or one app's set, and imports other people's. Your licence is never included.
  • You can remove personal details (paths, pasted text, commands) before sharing, and importing warns about buttons that run shell commands.
  • Importing merges instead of piling up copies.
