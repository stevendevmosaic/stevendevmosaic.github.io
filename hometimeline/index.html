Home Timeline

See what your home is about to do.

Apple Home shows you your automations, but not what will actually happen, and when. Home Timeline lists every change for the next 48 hours in order, accessory by accessory, and lets you run any of it right now.

What it does

  • A timeline of the next 48 hours. Sunsets, times of day and weekdays worked out for you. Changes drop off as they run.
  • Live status. Each accessory shows on now, off now, or no response when it is unplugged or out of range.
  • Run it now. Switch a light off early, run a scene, or do what a button press would do.
  • Every device in one list, including buttons and sensors that have no schedule, with a problems filter.
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen showing what happens next.

Private by design

There is no account and nothing is collected. Your Home is read on your own device and never leaves it. Read the privacy policy.

Works with

iPhone, iPad and Mac, with the automations you already have in Apple Home. You can try it first with a built-in sample home.
