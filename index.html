Two small apps, made with care

Mosaic Collage Maker

Turn a folder of photos into print-ready photobook pages — laid out automatically, at any page size, with every photo placed.

  • No fixed grid — every page is arranged differently
  • Any page size in millimetres, at 300 DPI
  • Exports JPEGs or a print-ready PDF for your photobook service
  • Works entirely offline

They work together

Journal in Daykeep  →  export to Mosaic Collage Maker  →  print a book.
Keep a trip or a year in Daykeep, then hand the whole journal — photos, captions and writing — to Mosaic Collage Maker, where it becomes an editable, print-ready photobook you can order anywhere.
