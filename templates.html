Templates

A template is a ready-made layout — frame arrangement, text and pinned branding, but no photos — that you download and fill with your own library.

Film contact-sheet packs

The first packs turn your photos into an authentic 35 mm contact sheet — cut film strips, sprocket holes and frame numbers. Each pack gives you a spread of grid densities and print sizes (A3, A4, A5 and Letter), so you just pick one, load your photos, and choose From Template.
