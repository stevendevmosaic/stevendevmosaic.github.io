Templates

A template is a ready-made layout — frame arrangement, text and pinned branding, but no photos — that you download and fill with your own library.

Film contact-sheet packs

The first packs turn your photos into an authentic 35 mm contact sheet — cut film strips, sprocket holes and frame numbers. Each pack gives you a spread of grid densities and print sizes (A3, A4, A5 and Letter), so you just pick one, load your photos, and choose From Template.

Polaroid scatter packs

New with the latest update: scatter your photos across the page as little instant-camera prints — white-framed, gently tilted, and captioned in a handwriting font. Load your photos, choose From Template, and reshuffle the pile until it feels right.

It's one generous pack: four surfaces (linen, wood, kraft and dark slate), every print size (A3, A4 and Letter in portrait and landscape, plus square) and three grid densities. Handwritten captions come switched on and ready to fill in. Prefer your own look? Import any background and the app-drawn frames stay crisp on top.
